# Storyline of Saga of James of Penrhyn

This document is the single source of truth for storyline of the campaign and it is maintained by the project's loremaster(s). Some parts are vague because they were not decided yet. Once the details are determined, they will written down here.

DO NOT read further if you do not want spoilers.

## Volume I.

The story begins when James' home village is attacked once again by some bandits. They are usually driven away by local militia with help of hunters but now, the latter have not returned from hunting yet. James offers to help in the fight but the leader of the militia, Ligwyn rather than letting the mage directly take part in the battle asks him to gather villagers, equip them with weapons and lead them. James, even though having some doubts, does as he is told. They are able to hold the bandits until a part of the hunting party returns. After winning the battle the hunters claim to not know where exactly the rest of the party is but they are able to provide some hints. Next morning is James sent with some villagers to find and bring them back.

The party is soon ambushed by the rest of the bandits band, led by the former leader's brother. Realizing that his brother was killed by the villagers, he seeks revenge for him but after some fighting he meets the same fate as his brother. Other bandits in the wood find out about it and avoid the party. Later when trying to find again the tracks of the hunting party, they are approached by a woman living here of name Belinda.

She offers to help the party in their search and warns them that this wood is inhabited by elves and they do not want people of other races here. James tries to persuade the elves to let them pass but he is unsuccessful. Surprisingly, the elves are attacked by a bigger group of orcs. James volunteers to assist the elves. They accept the offer, not caring for the humans’ lives. Together they defeat the invaders and the party is allowed to pass elves' wood.

After that the party arrives to a smaller human settlement which was recently overrun by orcs. Local hunters wanted to fight them back but the enemy was too numerous and could defeat them with ease in a regular encounter. Instead, they decided to trouble the orcs with small ambushes, often under the veil of night. Their actions have success, the orcs are confused and do not dare to continue in their conquest but they are not going to leave this land for good. The locals confirm that the hunting party went to this area and was captured by the orcs.

James decides to help them to drive away orcs and rescue his village's hunters. But firstly, they need to defeat their troll, goblin and naga allies. Trolls and goblins are in the village itself and are immediately taken care of. Then the party goes after nagas who inhabit a nearby river. They set up an ambush and kill their leader. After that they return to the village to fight the orcs. James and the partisans’ leader decide to ambush one group of enemy soldiers at a time and then challenge their leader.

In the end, they win the battle. The locals are to celebrate their regained freedom and invite James to join. He declines, claiming that he needs to return to his village with their hunters and bids them farewell. Belinda decides to stay with the party until they return home and guide them on the journey. It is peaceful until they approach the village’s graveyard. The dead have just started to raise from their graves and attack the villagers. The party joins the villagers and James’s brother and together take care of them.

After the battle Belinda expresses her feelings for James and her desire to stay in the village with him. He eventually manages to say that he feels the same and they proclaim themselves husband and wife. James’ brother returns to the earl’s army as his vacation is over. James gives Ligwyn a report about his mission. Ligwyn is concerned about orcish attacks and starts planning how to defend the village from their potential attack.

A few weeks later, he summons James and tasks him with buying better weapons from the dwarves. Belinda and some villagers join him on the mission. The party is attacked by a few small bands of outlaws and later orcs but those are quickly defeated. Eventually, they make it to the Dwarven Doors.

Unfortunately, the city is besieged by an orcish army. After he helps break the siege, he is invited to the city and directed to a dwarvish smith. They make a deal and the party returns to their village. After everything is delivered to the village, James is praised for successfully finishing the mission. But during the wait he realized that while he finds helping other people fulfilling, he will never fully fit in with other people and prefers solitude (or company of just a few people).

So after the villagers are equipped to protect themselves from potential attacks, James leaves the village with his wife and the mages who he trained to look for a place for themselves. After some time of wandering, he encounters a former fellow student from the Academy, Guiscard, who agrees to travel with the party for a while.

Eventually they find a perfect place for the couple but they realize that it is occupied by a dragon and his drakes who attack the party. Amid the fight, James manages to convince the dragon that he means no harms and just wants to live there peacefully with his wife. The dragon agrees to let them settle there in exchange for their service in the future and entrusts a powerful artifact Rod of Hope to James.

Sometime after they settle, his home village is once again attacked by a powerful necromancer which they help fight but in the chaos his sister vanishes. He goes to look for her. He later finds out that she was kidnapped by that necromancer who was able to escape the battle. James vows to find and defeat the necromancer and save his sister before he dies.

## Volume II.

James is summoned by the dragon who tells him about strange things happening in underground channels near Knalga. James agrees to help investigate and resolve those issues. He calls upon his former students and together with his wife set out for Knalga. On the way they meet a white mage Cateline who agrees to help with the mission.

After they reach the city, they arrange a meeting with the local lord. He has heard of James and gladly accepts the mage's help and even sends some dwarves with the party. The dwarves lead the party through the caverns to the source of the happenings.

Along the way, they have to fight trolls, undead and some fire elementals. Unexpectedly Belinda falls to a chasm, no one is able to save her. Eventually the rest of the party resolve the issues. James reports that to the dwarvish lord who in turn offers his condolences to James.

James returns to his place to bury his wife and mourns her.

## Volume III.

James is visited by an elementalist who has news of James' arch enemy (the necromancer who attacked his village and kidnapped his sister). The necromancer plans to steal an ancient artifact which would allow him to cause a cataclysm. James agrees to help the elementalist to stop his enemy.

On the way to the location of the artifact, the elementalist teaches James new spells and how to summon elementals. They encounter some enemies but quickly deal with them thanks to their elementals.

Once they found the artifact, the elementalist reveals that he actually serves James' enemy and wanted to use James to obtain the artifact for his master. James does not want to believe that.

A battle ensues between the two elemental wizards. They are evenly matched. James is only able to defeat his adversary by sacrificing the Rod of Hope. After his victory he again hides the artifact and puts protective spells around it.

When the artifact is protected again, James returns to his place to inform the dragon of the fate of the rod entrusted to him. He apologies for destroying it but the dragon tells him that it was meant to be used this way when all hope is lost. That lifts the mage's spirits a bit.
